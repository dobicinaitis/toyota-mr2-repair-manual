# Maintenance operations

## Engine

Cold Engine Operations

### Replace timing belt

1. Remove the timing belt.
    * 3S-GTE (See pages EM-26 to 31)
    * 5S-FE (See pages EM-47 to 52)
2. Install the timing belt.
    * 3S-GTE (See pages EM-35 to 41)
    * 5S-FE (See pages EM-55 to 60)

### Inspect drive belt

1.  Visually check the drive belt for excessive wear, frayed cords etc. If necessary, replace the drive belt.

    :material-lightbulb: **HINT**: Cracks on the ribbed side of a drive belt are considered acceptable. If the drive
    belt
    has chunks missing from the ribs, it should be replaced.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CH0004_CH0752.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>
    <br/>

2.  Using a belt tension gauge, measure the drive belt tension.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/EC0003_EC0004_EC0001.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    Belt tension gauge:

    * Nippondenso BTG-20 (95506-00020)
    * Borroughs No.BT-33-73F

    Drive belt tension:

    * Alternator
        * New belt - 120 + 20 Ib
        * Used belt - 104 + 20 Ib
    * A/C compressor
        * New belt - 160 + 25 Ib
        * Used belt - 100 + 20 Ib

    If necessary, adjust the drive belt tension.

    :material-lightbulb: **HINT**:

    * "New belt" refers to a belt which has been used 5 minutes or less on a running engine.
    * "Used belt" refers to a belt which has been used on a running engine for 5 minutes or more.
    * After installing the belt, check that it fits properly in the tibbed grooves.
    * Check by hand to confirm that the belt has not slipped out of the groove on the bottom of the pulley.
    * After installing a new belt, run the engine for about 5 minutes and recheck the belt tension.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CH0087.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

### Inspect air filter

1.  Visually check that the air cleaner element is not excessively, damaged or oily.
    
    If necessary, replace the air cleaner element.

2.  Clean the element with compressed air.

    First blow from the inside thoroughly, then blow off the outside of the element.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/MA0061.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

### Replace air filter

Replace the air cleaner element with a new one.
