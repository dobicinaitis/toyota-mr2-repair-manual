## Removal of oil cooler

1.  Disconnect cable from negative terminal of battery.

    !!! warning "Caution"

        Work must be started after approx. 20 seconds or longer from the time the ignition switch is turned to the
        `LOCK` position and the negative (`–`) terminal cable is disconnected from the battery.

2.  Drain engine coolant. (See page CO-6)
3.  Remove engine under covers.
4.  Remove RH engine hood side panel.
5.  Remove No.1 air intake connector. (See [Intercooler, step 4](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#intercooler))
6.  Remove A/C compressor without disconnecting hoses. (See [Intercooler › Removal of intercooler, steps 10–12](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#p-tc-21))
7.  Remove oil filter. (See [Replacement of engine oil and oil filter](replacement-of-engine-oil-and-oil-filter.md#replacement-of-engine-oil-and-oil-filter))
8.  [](){ #p-lu-19 } Disconnect oil dipstick guide from water inlet.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1050.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

9.  Disconnect water by-pass hose from oil cooler.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1051.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

10. Remove oil cooler.

    1.  Remove the relief valve and plate washer.
    2.  Remove the two nuts, oil cooler and gasket.
    3.  Remove the O-ring and gasket from the oil cooler.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1052.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    4.  Remove the bolt, and disconnect the water by-pass pipe from the oil cooler bracket.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1053.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

11. Remove oil cooler bracket.

    1. Remove the four bolts and oil cooler bracket.
    2. Remove the two O-rings from the oil cooler.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1054.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

## Inspection of oil cooler

1.  Inspect relief valve.

    Push the valve with a wooden stick to check if it is stuck.

    If stuck, replace the relief valve.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0933.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Inspect oil cooler.

    Check the oil cooler for damage or clogging.

    If necessary, replace the oil cooler.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0942.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

## Installation of oil cooler

(See [Oil cooler (3S-GTE)](#oil-cooler-3s-gte))

1.  Install oil cooler bracket.

    1.  Install two new O-rings to the oil cooler bracket.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0920.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    2.  Apply a light coat of engine oil on the small O-ring.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0921.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    3.  Install the oil cooler bracket with the four bolts.

        **Torque:** 80 kg-cm (69 in.-lb, 7.8 N·m)

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1054.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

2.  [](){ #p-lu-21 } Install oil cooler.

    1.  Install new O-ring and gasket to the oil cooler.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1055.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    2.  Temporarily install the water by-pass pipe with the bolt.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1057.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    3.  Apply a light coat of engine oil on the threads and under the head of the relief valve.
    4.  Place a new gasket on the water by-pass pipe.
    5.  Temporarily install the oil cooler with the plate washer, relief valve and two nuts.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1058.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    6.  Tighten the relief valve.

        **Torque:** 800 kg-cm (58 ft-lb, 78 N·m)

    7.  Tighten the two nuts holding the oil cooler to the water by-pass pipe.

        **Torque:** 120 kg-cm (9 ft-lb, 12 N·m)

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1059.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    8.  Tighten the bolt holding the water by-pass pipe to oil cooler bracket.

        **Torque:** 180 kg-cm (13 ft-lb, 18 N·m)

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1060.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

3.  [](){ #p-lu-22 } Connect water by-pass hose to oil cooler.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1051.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

4.  Install oil dipstick guide to water inlet.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1050.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

5.  Install oil filter. (See [Replacement of engine oil and oil filter](replacement-of-engine-oil-and-oil-filter.md#replacement-of-engine-oil-and-oil-filter))
6.  Install A/C compressor. (See [Intercooler › Installation of intercooler, steps 3–5](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#installation-of-intercooler))
7.  Install No.1 air intake connector. (See [Intercooler › Installation of intercooler, step 10](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#p-tc-25))
8.  Install RH engine hood side panel.
9.  Connect cable to negative terminal of battery.
10. Fill with engine coolant. (See page CO-7)
11. Start engine and check for leaks.
12. Check engine oil level. (See [Oil pressure check](oil-pressure-check.md#oil-pressure-check))
13. Install engine under covers.
