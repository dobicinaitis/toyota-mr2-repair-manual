# Oil pump

## Removal of oil pump

!!! tip "Hint"

    When repairing the oil pump, the oil pan and strainer should be removed and cleaned.

<figure markdown="span">
  ![](images/LU1074.webp#illustration)
</figure>

<figure markdown="span">
  ![](images/LU1083.webp#illustration)
</figure>

1.  [](){ #p-lu-10 } Disconnect cable from negative terminal of battery.

    !!! warning "Caution"

        Work must be started after approx. 20 seconds or longer from the time the ignition switch is turned to the
        `LOCK` position and the negative (`–`) terminal cable is disconnected from the battery.

2.  Drain engine oil. (See [Oil pressure check](oil-pressure-check.md#oil-pressure-check))
3.  Remove engine under covers.
4.  Remove RH engine hood side panel.
5.  Remove suspension upper brace.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Removal of engine, step 8](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#removal-of-engine))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Removal of engine, step 8](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#removal-of-engine))

6.  Remove cruise control actuator (w/ cruise control system) and accelerator linkage.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Removal of engine, step 13](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#p-em-135))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Removal of engine, step 11](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#removal-of-engine))

7.  Remove front exhaust pipe.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Removal of engine, steps 30, 31](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#p-em-138))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Removal of engine, step 29](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-187))

8.  (3S-GTE) Remove A/C compressor without disconnecting hoses. (See [Intercooler › Removal of intercooler, step 12](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#p-tc-21))
9.  (5S-FE) Remove A/C belt idler pulley. (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Removal of engine, step 31](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-187))
10. (3S-GTE) Remove catalytic converter. (See [Cylinder head (3S-GTE) › Removal of cylinder head, step 14](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-head-3s-gte.md#p-em-64))
11. (3S-GTE) Remove intercooler. (See [Intercooler, steps 4, 5, 7–13](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#intercooler))
12. Remove stiffener plate.

    Remove the three bolts and stiffener plate.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1005.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

13. [](){ #p-lu-11 } Disconnect turbocharger oil outlet hose from oil pan.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1082.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

14. Remove oil pan.

    1.  Remove the dipstick.
    2.  Remove the seventeen bolts and two nuts.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1008.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    3.  Insert the blade of SST between the cylinder block and oil pan, cut off applied sealer and remove the oil pan.

        **SST** `09032-00100`

        !!! note "Notice"

            * Do not use SST for the oil pump body side and rear oil seal retainer.
            * Be careful not to damage the oil pan flange.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0246.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

15. Remove oil strainer and baffle plate.

    Remove the two bolts, two nuts, oil strainer, baffle plate and gasket.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0889.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

16. Suspend engine with engine chain hoist.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1069.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

17. Remove timing belt.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Timing belt (3S-GTE), steps 7, 8, 10–22](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-3s-gte.md#timing-belt-3s-gte))
    * 5S-FE (See [Timing belt (5S-FE), steps 6–8, 10–21](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-5s-fe.md#timing-belt-5s-fe))

18. Remove No.2 idler pulley, crankshaft timing pulley and oil pump pulley.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Timing belt (3S-GTE) › Adjustment of valve timing, steps 24, 24](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-3s-gte.md#adjustment-of-valve-timing))
    * 5S-FE (See [Timing belt (3S-GTE) › Adjustment of valve timing, steps 24, 24](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-3s-gte.md#adjustment-of-valve-timing))

19. [](){ #p-lu-12 } Remove oil pump.

    1.  Remove the twelve bolts.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1049.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    2.  Using a plastic-faced, remove the oil pump by careful tapping the oil pump body.
    3.  Remove the gasket.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0890.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

## Disassembly of oil pump

(See [Removal of oil pump](#p-lu-12))

1.  Remove relief valve.

    1. Using snap ring pliers, remove the snap ring.
    2. Remove the retainer, spring and relief valve.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0431.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Remove drive and driven rotors.

    Remove the two bolts, pump body cover, O-ring, the drive and driven rotors.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0903.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

## Inspection of oil pump

1.  Inspect relief valve.

    Coat the valve with engine oil and check that it falls smoothly into the valve hole by its own weight.

    If it doesn't, replace the relief valve. If necessary, replace the oil pump assembly.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0252.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Inspect drive and driven rotors.

    **A. Inspect rotor body clearance**

    Using a feeler gauge, measure the clearance between the driven rotor and body.

    **Standard body clearance:** 0.100 – 0.160 mm (0.0039 – 0.0063 in.)

    **Maximum body clearance:** 0.20 mm (0.0079 in.)

    If the body clearance is greater than maximum, replace the rotors as a set. If necessary, replace the oil pump
    assembly.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0891.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

    **B. Inspect rotor tip clearance**

    Using a feeler gauge, measure the clearance between the drive and driven rotors.

    **Standard tip clearance:** 0.040 – 0.160 mm (0.0016 – 0.0063 in.)

    **Maximum tip clearance:** 0.20 mm (0.0079 in.)

    If the tip clearance is greater than maximum, replace the rotors as a set.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0892.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

[](){ #p-lu-14 }
## Replacement of oil pump oil seal

1.  Remove oil seal.

    Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil seal.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0893.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Install oil seal.

    1.  Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal until its surface is flush with the oil pump cover edge.

        **SST** `09620-30010` (`09627-30010`, `09631-00020`)

    2.  Apply MP grease to the oil seal lip.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0894.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

## Replacement of crankshaft front oil seal

(See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Replacement of crankshaft oil seals](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#replacement-of-crankshaft-oil-seals))

## Assembly of oil pump

(See [Removal of oil pump](#p-lu-12))

1.  Install drive and driven rotors.

    1.  Place the drive and driven rotors into pump body with the marks facing the pump body cover side.
    2.  Install a new O-ring to the pump body.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0895.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    3.  Install the pump body cover with the two bolts.

        **Torque:** 90 kg-cm (78 in.-lb, 8.8 N·m)

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0903.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

2.  Install relief valve.

    1. Insert the relief valve, spring and retainer into the pump body hole.
    2. Using snap ring pliers, install the snap ring.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0431.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

[](){ #p-lu-15 }
## Installation of oil pump

(See [Oil pump](#oil-pump))

1.  Install oil pump.

    Install a new gasket and the oil pump with the twelve bolts.

    **Torque:**

    * 3S-GTE – 80 kg-cm (69 in.-lb, 7.8 N·m)
    * 5S-FE – 95 kg-cm (82 in.-lb, 9.3 N·m)

    !!! tip "Hint"

        Each bolt length is indicated in the figure.

    **Bolt length:**

    * Long bolt – 35 mm (1.38 in.)
    * Others – 25 mm (0.98 in.)

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1049-long-bolt.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Install oil pump pulley, crankshaft timing pulley and No.2 idler pulley.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Timing belt (3S-GTE) › Installation of timing belt, steps 1–3](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-3s-gte.md#installation-of-timing-belt))
    * 5S-FE (See [Timing belt (5S-FE) › Installation of timing belt, steps 1–3](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-5s-fe.md#installation-of-timing-belt))

3.  Install timing belt.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Timing belt (3S-GTE) › Installation of timing belt, steps 5–8, 10–25](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-3s-gte.md#installation-of-timing-belt))
    * 5S-FE (See [Timing belt (5S-FE) › Installation of timing belt, steps 5–8, 10–20](../engine-mechanical/timing-belt-5s-fe.md#installation-of-timing-belt))

4.  Remove engine chain hoist from engine.
5.  Install baffle plate and oil strainer.

    Install a new gasket, the baffle plate and the oil strainer with the two bolts and two nuts.

    **Torque:** 55 kg-cm (48 in.-lb, 5.4 N·m)

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU0889.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

6.  Install oil pan.

    1.  Remove any old packing (FIPG) material and be careful not to drop any oil on the contact surfaces of the oil
        pan and cylinder block.

        * Using a razor blade and gasket scraper, remove all the old packing (FIPG) material from the gasket surfaces
          and sealing groove.
        * Thoroughly clean all components to remove all the loose material.
        * Using a non-residue solvent, clean both sealing surfaces.

        !!! note "Notice"

            Do not use a solvent which will affect the painted surfaces.

    2.  [](){ #p-lu-16 } Apply seal packing to the oil pan as shown in the figure.

        **Seal packing:** Part No. `08826-00080` or equivalent

        * Install a nozzle that has been cut to a 3 – 5 mm (0.12 – 0.20 in.) opening.

        !!! tip "Hint"

            Avoid applying an excessive amount to the surface. Be particularly careful near oil passages.

        * Parts must be assembled within 5 minutes of application. Otherwise the material must be removed and
          reapplied.
        * Immediately remove nozzle from the tube and reinstall cap.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU0420.webp#illustration){ width="60%" }
        </figure>

    3.  Install the oil pan with the seventeen bolts and four nuts.

        **Torque:** 55 kg-cm (48 in.-lb, 5.4 N·m)

    4.  Install the dipstick.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/LU1008.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

7.  Connect turbocharger oil outlet hose to oil pan.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1082.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

8.  Install stiffener plate.

    Install the stiffener plate with the two bolts.

    **Torque:** 380 kg-cm (27 ft-lb, 37 N·m)

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/LU1005.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

9.  [](){ #p-lu-17 } (3S-GTE) Install intercooler. (See [Intercooler › Installation of intercooler, steps 2–8, 10, 11](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#installation-of-intercooler))
10. (3S-GTE) Install catalytic converter. (See [Cylinder head (3S-GTE) › Installation of cylinder head, step 28](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-head-3s-gte.md#p-em-94))
11. (3S-GTE) Install A/C compressor. (See [Intercooler › Installation of intercooler, step 3](../turbocharger-system/intercooler.md#installation-of-intercooler))
12. (5S-FE) Install A/C belt idler pulley. (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Installation of engine, step 13](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-221))
13. Install front exhaust pipe.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Installation of engine, steps 18, 19](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#p-em-174))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Installation of engine, step 15](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-221))

14. Install cruise control actuator (w/ cruise control system) and accelerator linkage.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Installation of engine, step 36](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#p-em-178))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Installation of engine, step 33](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-226))

15. Install suspension upper brace.

    * 3S-GTE (See [Cylinder block (3S-GTE) › Installation of engine, step 41](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-3s-gte.md#p-em-179))
    * 5S-FE (See [Cylinder block (5S-FE) › Installation of engine, step 36](../engine-mechanical/cylinder-block-5s-fe.md#p-em-226))

16. Connect cable to negative terminal of battery.
17. Fill with engine oil. (See [Replacement of engine oil and oil filter](replacement-of-engine-oil-and-oil-filter.md#p-lu-8))
18. Start engine and check for leaks.
19. Recheck engine oil level. (See [Oil pressure check](oil-pressure-check.md#oil-pressure-check))
20. Install RH engine hood side panel.
