# Distributor (5S-FE)

## Removal of distributor

<figure markdown="span">
  ![](images/IG1375.webp#illustration)
</figure>

1.  **Disconnect cable from negative terminal of battery**

    !!! warning "Caution"

        Work must be started after approx. 20 seconds or longer from the time the ignition switch is turned to the
        "LOCK" position and the negative (–) terminal cable is disconnected from the battery.

2.  **Disconnect distributor connector**
3.  **Disconnect high-tension cord from ignition coil** (See [Distributor (3S-GTE), step 3](distributor-3s-gte.md#distributor-3s-gte))
4.  **Disconnect high-tension cords from spark plugs**

    1.  Disconnect the cord clamp from the engine hanger.
    2.  Disconnect the four high-tension cords from the spark plugs. (See [On-vehicle inspection (5S-FE) › Inspection of high-tension cords](on-vehicle-inspection-5s-fe.md#inspection-of-high-tension-cords))

5.  [](){ #p-ig-20 } **Remove distributor**

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/IG1365.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    1.  Remove the hold-down bolt and pull out the distributor.
    2.  Remove the O-ring from the distributor housing.

## Installation of distributor

1.  **Remove engine under covers**
2.  **Remove RH engine hood side panel**
3.  **Set No.1 cylinder to TDC/compression**

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/IG1385_IG6428.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    Turn the crankshaft clockwise, and position the slit of the intake camshaft as shown.

4.  **Install distributor**

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/IG1105.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    1.  Install a new O-ring to the housing.
    2.  Apply a light coat of engine oil on the O-ring.
    3.  Align the cutout portion of the coupling with the groove of the housing.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/IG1389.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
        </figure>

    4.  Insert the distributor, aligning the center of the flange with that of the bolt hole on the cylinder head.
    5.  Lightly tighten the hold-down bolt.
    6.  Install the high-tension cord clamp to the rear engine hanger.

5.  **Connect high-tension cord to ignition coil** (See [Distributor (3S-GTE) › Installation of distributor, step 5](distributor-3s-gte.md#installation-of-distributor))
6.  **Connect high-tension cords to spark plugs**

    **Firing order:** 1 – 3 – 4 – 2

7.  **Connect distributor connector**
8.  **Connect cable to negative terminal of battery**
9.  **Warm up engine**

    Allow the engine to warm up to normal operating temperature.

10. **Connect tachometer**

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/FI5722.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    Connect the test probe of a tachometer to terminal IG ⊖ of the check connector.

    !!! note "Notice"

        * NEVER allow the tachometer terminal to touch ground as it could result in damage to the igniter and/or
          ignition coil.
        * As some tachometers are not compatible with this ignition system, we recommend that you confirm the
          compatibility of yours before use.

11. [](){ #p-ig-22 } **Adjust ignition timing**

    1.  Using SST, connect terminals TE1 and E1 of the check connector.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/FI5725.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
        </figure>

        **SST** `09843-18020`

        !!! tip "Hint"

            After engine rpm are kept at 1,000 – 1,300 rpm for 5 seconds, check that they return to idle speed.

    2.  Using a timing light, check the ignition timing.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/IG1388.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
        </figure>

        **Ignition timing:** 10° BTDC @ idle (Transmission in neutral range)

    3.  Loosen the hold-down bolt, and adjust by turning the distributor.
    4.  Tighten the hold-down bolt, and recheck the ignition timing.

        **Torque:** 130 kg-cm (9 ft-lb, 13 N·m)

    5.  Remove the SST.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/FI5702.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
        </figure>

        **SST** `09843-18020`

12. **Further check ignition timing**

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/IG1387.webp#illustration){ width="400px" }
    </figure>

    **Ignition timing:** 13 – 22° BTDC @ idle (Transmission in neutral range)

    !!! tip "Hint"

        The timing mark moves in a range between 13° and 22°.

13. **Disconnect tachometer and timing light from engine**
14. **Reinstall RH engine hood side panel**
15. **Reinstall engine under covers**
