# Thermostat

## Removal of thermostat

1.  Drain engine coolant. (See [Replacement of engine coolant › Drainage of engine coolant](replacement-of-engine-coolant.md#drainage-of-engine-coolant))
2.  Disconnect radiator hose from water inlet.
3.  (3S-GTE) Disconnect oil dipstick guide from water inlet. (See [Oil cooler (3S-GTE) › Removal of oil cooler, step 8](../lubrication-system/oil-cooler-3s-gte.md#p-lu-19))
4.  Remove water inlet and thermostat.

    1.  Remove the two nuts and water inlet from the water pump.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/CO0966.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    2.  Remove the thermostat.
    3.  Remove the gasket from the thermostat.

## Inspection of thermostat

### Inspect thermostat

!!! tip "Hint"

    The thermostat is numbered with the valve opening temperature.

1.  Immerse the thermostat in water and gradually heat the water.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO0081.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Check the valve opening temperature.

    **Valve opening temperature:** 80 – 84°C (176 – 183°F)

    If the valve opening temperature is not as specified, replace the thermostat.

3.  Check the valve lift.

    **Valve lift:** 8 mm (0.31 in.) or more at 95°C (203°F)

    If the valve lift is less than specification, replace the thermostat.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO0032.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

4.  Check that the valve spring is tight when the thermostat is fully closed.

    If necessary, replace the thermostat.

## Installation of thermostat

1.  Place thermostat in water pump.

    1.  Install a new gasket to the thermostat.
    2.  Align the jiggle valve of the thermostat with the upper side of the stud bolt, and insert the thermostat
        in the water pump.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/CO0937.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

        !!! tip "Hint"

            The jiggle valve may be set within 5° of either side of the prescribed position.

2.  Install water inlet.

    Install the water inlet with the two nuts.

    **Torque:**

    * 3S-GTE – 80 kg-cm (69 in.-lb, 7.8 N·m)
    * 5S-FE – 90 kg-cm (78 in.-lb, 8.8 N·m)

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO0966.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

3.  (3S-GTE) Connect oil dipstick guide to water inlet. (See [Oil cooler (3S-GTE) › Installation of oil cooler, step 4](../lubrication-system/oil-cooler-3s-gte.md#p-lu-22))
4.  Connect radiator hose to water inlet.
5.  Fill with engine coolant. (See [Replacement of engine coolant › Refill of engine coolant](replacement-of-engine-coolant.md#refill-of-engine-coolant))
6.  Start engine and check for leaks.
