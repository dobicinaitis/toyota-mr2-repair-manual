## On-vehicle inspection

### Low temperature (below 83°C (181°F))

1.  Turn ignition switch "ON".

    Check that the cooling fan stops.

    If not, check the cooling fan relays and water temperature switch, and check for a separated connector or
    severed wire between the cooling fan relay and water temperature switch.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO1122_CO1127.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

2.  Disconnect water temperature switch connector.

    Check that the cooling fan rotates.

    If not, check the fan main relay, cooling fan relays, cooling fan and fuses, and check for a short circuit
    between the cooling fan relay and water temperature switch.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO1129.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

3.  Connect water temperature switch connector.

### High temperature (above 93°C (199°F))

4.  Start engine.

    1.  Raise coolant temperature to above 93°C (199°F).
    2.  Check that the cooling fan rotates.

        <figure markdown="span">
          ![](images/CO1108_CO1128.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
        </figure>

    If not, replace the water temperature switch.

## Inspection of radiator cooling fan components

1.  Inspect fan main relay ("FAN MAIN"). (See [Ignition main relay "IGN"](../../charging-system/ignition-main-relay.md#ignition-main-relay-ign))

    Check the relay in the same way as the Ignition Main Relay.

2.  Inspect No.1 cooling fan relay ("FAN NO.1"). (See [Radiator cooling fans (w/ A/C) › Inspection of radiator cooling fan components](radiator-cooling-fans-w-a-c.md#p-co-23))
3.  Inspect radiator water temperature switch.

    1.  Using an ohmmeter, check that there is no continuity between the terminals when the coolant temperature
        is above 93°C (199°F).
    2.  Using an ohmmeter, check that there is continuity between the terminals when the coolant temperature is
        below 83°C (181°F).

    If continuity is not as specified, replace the switch.

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/AC0536.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>

4.  [](){ #p-co-30 } Inspect No.1 radiator cooling fan.

    1.  Connect battery and ammeter to the cooling fan connector.
    2.  Check that the cooling fan rotates smoothly, and check the reading on the ammeter.

    **Standard amperage:**

    * M/T – 5.8 – 7.4 A
    * A/T – 8.8 – 10.8 A

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![](images/CO1118.webp#illustration){ width="80%" }
    </figure>
